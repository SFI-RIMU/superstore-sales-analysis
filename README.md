# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis

**A Data Analytics Internship Project using Python**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify business trends, evaluate profitability, and generate actionable insights. It was completed as part of a hands-on Data Analytics internship.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Explore monthly sales trends and potential seasonality.
- Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Evaluate regional performance and customer segments.
- Present findings through data visualizations.

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Overall Performance:** Total sales were approximately $2.30 million, with total profit of $286,397.
- **Category Performance:** Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three product categories.
- **Furniture Profitability:** Furniture had relatively high sales but much lower profit than Technology and Office Supplies.
- **Discount Analysis:** Several higher discount levels were associated with negative total profit.
- **Regional Performance:** The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
- **Customer Segments:** The Consumer segment contributed the highest total sales and profit.

## 📂 Project Files

- `superstore_analysis.ipynb` — Data cleaning, analysis, visualizations, and business findings.
- `superstore_sales.csv` — Dataset used for the analysis.
